## Summary

What to do in your first few sessions, and the order to do it in.

## Recommendation

Stabilise the economy before expanding. The common early mistake is pushing
territory or roster breadth while medicine production still cannot keep up
with infected intake — which turns every Rat Swarm into an emergency instead
of a scheduled event.

Rough order of operations:

1. Get medicine production self-sustaining (herb cultivation → crafting →
   ward throughput).
2. Bring ward capacity in line with survivor rescue rate.
3. Only then widen the hero roster or push territory.

## Details

Last Asylum: Plague layers three systems that compete for the same
resources:

- **Sanctuary management** — rescuing survivors, upgrading medical wards,
  cultivating herbs, crafting medicine.
- **Tower-defense waves** — Rat Swarms arriving on a timer, escalating to
  Rat King boss encounters.
- **Tactical squad combat** — hero squads used for territory and city
  conquest.

Progress stalls when these are funded evenly. Fund the sanctuary first: it
is the only one of the three that *generates* rather than consumes.
